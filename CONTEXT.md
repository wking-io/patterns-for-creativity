# Patterns for Creativity

This presentation explains a practical model for recognizing and cultivating the conditions that produce creative ideas.

## Language

**From the work**:
A work-derived catalyst that surfaces through making, using, testing, or sharing the current project; feedback and friction are its common forms.
_Avoid_: Internal catalyst, direct catalyst

**From the world**:
A world-derived catalyst that begins in an experience beyond the current project and becomes relevant when it collides with the work.
_Avoid_: External catalyst, exposure catalyst
