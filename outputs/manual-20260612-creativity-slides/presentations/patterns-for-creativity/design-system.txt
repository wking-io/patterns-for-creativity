slide size: 1280x720

background system:
- Deep ink base (#08111F) for argument and section slides.
- Warm paper base (#F6EFE2) for pattern/tool slides.
- Occasional split backgrounds for contrast.

typography:
- Titles: Aptos Display, 44-64px, bold.
- Section claims: Aptos Display, 62-76px, bold.
- Body/labels: Aptos, 16-26px.
- Small notes/footers: Aptos, 10-12px.

palette:
- Ink: #08111F
- Paper: #F6EFE2
- Bone: #FFF8EA
- Amber: #F5B85C
- Cyan: #54C7D4
- Coral: #F16D5B
- Green: #86C26A
- Muted line: #8B96A8

chart and diagram grammar:
- Use editable shapes with direct labels.
- Use amber for creativity, cyan for AI/factory, coral for friction/unknowns, green for signal/learning.
- Lines are hairline or 2px; arrows only when direction matters.

container grammar:
- Rounded rectangles only for lanes, placeholders, or grouped model components.
- No decorative card grids.

source/footer grammar:
- Bottom-left slide number and short source note at 10px.
- Footer is quiet and omitted on cover/section reset slides when visual purity helps.

title/kicker grammar:
- Kicker in uppercase 11px, amber or muted ink.
- Claim title is a sentence with a conclusion.

layout families:
- Cover contrast
- Loop/flow diagram
- Matrix
- Split contrast
- Quote
- Two-axis model
- Placeholder asset stage
- Section reset
- Process path
- Closing thesis

banned motifs:
- Generic AI sparkles.
- Decorative blobs/orbs.
- Fake logos or app icons.
- Dense paragraph slides.
