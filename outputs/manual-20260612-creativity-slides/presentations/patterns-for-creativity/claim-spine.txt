thesis:
AI is best at work with known outcomes, so human leverage shifts toward creativity: the practice of discovering what is worth making.

Designers, engineers, product builders, and creative technologists navigating AI-assisted work.

one-line arc:
Separate manufacturing from creativity, then show the patterns that make creativity deliberate: notice, collect, broaden experience, deepen expertise, explore cheaply, and shorten feedback loops.
